# Netflix-Movies-TV-Shows-Analysis
An end-to-end data analysis project on the Netflix Titles dataset, covering data cleaning, exploration, and visualization in Python, equivalent exploratory querying in SQL, and a summary Excel dashboard.

Project Overview

This project explores the Netflix catalog (movies and TV shows) to uncover trends in content type, release patterns, countries, genres, ratings, and directors. The same core questions are answered three ways — using Pandas/Matplotlib, SQL, and Excel — to demonstrate the full analytics toolkit used in a typical data analyst workflow.

What the Analysis Covers

Movies vs TV Shows — overall content type split
Content Added Per Year — growth of the catalog over time (2008–2021)
Top 10 Countries — countries producing the most titles
Top 10 Genres — most common content categories
Ratings Distribution — breakdown by content rating (TV-MA, TV-14, R, etc.)
Top 10 Longest Movies — by runtime in minutes
Top 10 Directors — most frequently credited directors

Key Insights

The catalog is roughly 70% Movies and 30% TV Shows (6,131 vs 2,676 titles).
United States and India are by far the top content-producing countries, followed by the UK, Canada, and Japan.
Dramas and Comedies are the most common genres.
TV-MA and TV-14 are the most frequent ratings, indicating a catalog skewed toward mature/teen audiences.
Content additions peaked around 2019 (2,016 titles added), after several years of rapid growth from 2016 onward.

Tools & Technologies

Python: Pandas (data cleaning & aggregation), Matplotlib (visualization)
SQL: MySQL-style syntax for grouping, filtering, and ranking queries
Excel: Pivot tables and charts for a self-contained interactive dashboard
